# Third-party things

## Vadivam icons

The icons in `public/vadivam-icons.js` come from [Vadivam](https://github.com/praveenjuge/vadivam) by Praveen Juge, used under the MIT License (the license text is in `public/vadivam-LICENSE.txt`). Rebuild the file with `python3 tools/build_icons.py <vadivam checkout> <app> public/vadivam-icons.js`.

## Keydown, licenses

`public/Keydown.js` is Radii Office's own Keydown engine. The license texts in Mintcode's `public/licenses.json` are the standard texts from the SPDX license list.
